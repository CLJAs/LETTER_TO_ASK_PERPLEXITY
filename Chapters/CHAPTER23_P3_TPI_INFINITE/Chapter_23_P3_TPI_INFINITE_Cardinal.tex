\chapter{ TPI for INFINITE cardinals}

	\section{DEFINITION of TPI, when the two sets has infinite cardinal}
	
		\noindent
		A TPI is builded over a set DOMAIN, and a set ORIGIN.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		\underline{\textbf{FIRST REQUISITE:}}\\\\
		We need to create a partition of ORIGIN.\\\\
		1) We will create exactly $\aleph_{0}$ different subsets, inside the partition. No matter the cardinal of ORIGIN.\\\\
		2) We will call them UNIVERSES THETA, and we will assign to each one a natural subindex and an order: $\theta_{1}, \theta_{2}, \theta_{3}, .. , \theta_{k}, ..$.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		\underline{\textbf{SECOND REQUISITE:}}\\\\
		We will define an entire system of relations.\\\\
		1) All relations will use DOMAIN as their domain set.\\\\
		2) The system will have exactly $\aleph_{0}$ different relations, and they will be named as: $r_{\theta_{1}}, r_{\theta_{2}}, .. , r_{\theta_{k}}, ..$.\\\\ 
		3) Each $r_{\theta_{k}}$ relation will use the universe $\theta_{k}$ as their image set.\\\\
		4) All relations MUST BE COMPLETELY DEFINED until the last R-pair, and they need to be "complete relations"(each member of their domain will have an image, even if we need to cheat). THEY DON'T NEED TO BE FUNCTIONS.
		\\\\
		
		\newpage
		\noindent
		\underline{\textbf{THIRD REQUISITE:}}\\\\
		1) We will define a partition of (DOMAIN x DOMAIN).\\\\
		2) This partition will have exactly $\aleph_{0}$ different subsets of D-pairs. No matter the cardinal of DOMAIN.\\\\
		3) These members will be called Families, and they will have a natural subindex and an order assigned: $F_{0}, F_{1}, F_{2}, .. , F_{k-1}, F_{k}, F_{k+1}, ..$.\\\\
		4) They could have a special subindex. We can only have got a finite quantity of Families with special subindexes, and they must be in the initial positions of the order.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		\textbf{\underline{FOURTH REQUISITE:}}\\\\
		$$ 	NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k+1}}} \:\: \subset \:\: NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k}}} $$
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		\textbf{\underline{FIFTH REQUISITE: The psychological one}}\\\\
		For every two possible consecutive relations, $r_{\theta_{k}}$, ordered by the k subindex of the universe they use inside... we must have a "substancial improvement". This means that the difference between their NWSP sets, must be one or more Families of D-pairs:\\\\
		$$ NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k}}}  \:\:\: \setminus \:\:\:  NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k+1}}} $$
		1) The difference MUST be one or more Families.\\\\
		2) And the cardinal of the difference must be the same cardinal as DOMAIN.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		\underline{\textbf{SIXTH REQUISITE:}}\\\\
		$$ \bigcap_{k=1}^{\infty} NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k}}} = \emptyset $$
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		\textbf{If we have acomplished with ALL these six requeriments, we can say:}\\\\
		$$ Card(DOMAIN) \ngtr Card(ORIGIN) $$

	\section{Why are those 6 requisites for TPI, enough to the conclussion?}
	
		\noindent
		We have $\aleph_{0}$ disjoint universes (from ORIGIN).\\\\
		We have $\aleph_{0}$ disjoint Families  (from DOMAIN x DOMAIN).\\\\
		We have $\aleph_{0}$ relations $r_{\theta_{k}}$.\\\\
		And the perfect conditions to create a semi-xmas-tree pattern using them, but in the spectrum of D-pairs instead of elements.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		The infinite intersection of all sets $NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k}}}$ guarantee us we have shadowed all (DOMAIN x DOMAIN).\\\\
		No matter that none a singular $NWSP_{r_{\theta_{k}}}$ is empty: their intersection is. IT IS A REQUISITE: 6th\\\\
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		The Fourth and Fifth requisites give us a way to define STEPS, from an undefined initial situation, solving some families, to nothing less than the totallity of families. Because we KNOW we have shadowed all Families thanks to the sixth requisite.\\\\
		If you try to say a Family is not shadowed, they are ordered, and related to the first relation $r_{\theta_{k}}$ that quit it from NWSP. I have all relations previously defined and I know all theirs NWSP sets, measured in Families. Not only in quantites, we know the concrete list of entities too, to acomplish Fifth requisite.\\\\
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		Thanks that we made a partition for DOMAIN x DOMAIN in THIRD requisite: we know all Families, means all DOMAIN x DOMAIN.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		We haven't changed the cardinal of ORIGIN: in each relation we are using just disjoint subsets from a concrete partition of ORIGIN: First requisite.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		We can change from the perspective of elements to D-pairs solved, thanks to a system of relations, well designed, and the core of the definition of injectivity: 2nd requisite.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		We have translated, a mathematical construct, that in finite spectrum, and in the spectrum of D-pairs, means ORIGIN must have a worst case than only doubling the quantity of elements inside DOMAIN. And we don't have a few steps: we have $\aleph_{0}$ steps.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		But we have translated it to infinity, so we could say ORIGIN is bigger or EQUAL: saying ORIGIN is smaller, after being able to participate in the TPI... is an absurd.

	\section{WHY IS CALLED TPI?}
	
		\noindent
		TPI is written in spanish. It is it original name: "Transferencia de Pares Ilimitada".
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		In english means: Unlimited Transference of Pairs.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		We spend all the time TRANSFERING D-pairs from NWSP to WSP.
		\\\\
		
		\noindent
		It is UNLIMITED because our unique limit is "nothing less the cardinal of SNEIs".
