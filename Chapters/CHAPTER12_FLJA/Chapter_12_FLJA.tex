\chapter{FLJA\_Abstract: examples}

	\noindent
	Now I am going to provide a strange table. That table show HOW to create all R-pairs related to a concrete member of SNEIs. It is a list of members of $LCF_{2p}$, divided by columns. YOU MUST ADD to FLJA\_Abstract relation one R-pair per each member of $LCF_{2p}$ inside those infinite columns: always the same SNEI in the left, but different member of $LCF_{2p}$ in the right side.
	\\\\
